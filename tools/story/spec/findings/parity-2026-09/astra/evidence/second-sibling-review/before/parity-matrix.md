## Reading the matrix

Status describes completion of the **stated job**, not whether a similarly named API exists. `Partial` can mean a measured gap or only a verified subset; the reason is stated. `Unknown` means insufficient evidence, not absence. Evidence labels distinguish exercised behavior, source-traced implementation, and documentation. Discoverability and comparative merit are separate judgments.

No weighted percentage is reported. Six essential jobs and four important jobs are not interchangeable counters, and this research did not execute the entire protocol on every product. An essential transformation that produces a non-regression test cannot be offset by more toolbar options.

Storybook's core, its official integrations, independent integrations and hosted products are distinct providers. Ordinary integrations count toward a practical workflow, with their setup/cost attributed. No capability was credited merely because an addon name exists.
