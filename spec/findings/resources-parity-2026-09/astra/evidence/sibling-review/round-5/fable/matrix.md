## Controls (rubric §6)

- Refusal control R20 read OUT, not MATCH or WIN — the instrument is not blind to refusals.
- Known-capability control R2 read MATCH on exercised evidence, and the two conformance fixtures it names exist (six `resources-*` fixtures exist in total: dedupe-join, ensure-fresh-skip, owner-release-gc, refresh-error-keeps-data, scope-fail-closed, stale-reply-suppression).
- Fault controls went red where they should: a wrong article served (P1.13, TQ.18 both FAULT-DETECTED); a mutation that forgets its feed descriptor left the feed carrying the optimistic guess with no refetch (P2b.3 FAULT-DETECTED). A first attempt at that fault was masked by member tags (P2.8 FAULT-MISSED, recorded in `evidence.md` because it is itself a finding about the tag model).
- Request ledgers, not timeouts, back every "no duplicate fetch" and "refetched exactly these" claim (P1.19, P2.10, P3.12, TQ.20).
- Delayed and overlapping replies were produced by holding captured transport args (JVM) and pending promises (Node); no sleeps decide an outcome.
