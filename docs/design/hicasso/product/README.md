`decision-brief.md`, `specification.md` and everything under `lanes/` are the product's living normative records. They are amended in place, under beads, exactly like every other normative record in this repository. [Custody and amendment](#custody-and-amendment) is the rule to read before editing anything here, and it also records the destination split that ended this directory's interim custody — **five documents have left, and this address is now permanent for what remains**.

The three were first published here from an operator-local working set by `rf2-hic-000`: `decision-brief.md` from `synth.md`, `specification.md` from `synth-codex.md`, `lanes/` from `codex/`. That publication arrangement is **retired** (`rf2-rbme3`, 2026-08-16). What survives of it is the [publication record](#publication-record-historical), kept as provenance and nothing more.

The naming ledger is live and appended-to by beads (see rf2-hic-065), and it now lives at [`implementation/hicasso/spec/naming-ledger.md`](../../../../implementation/hicasso/spec/naming-ledger.md) with the rest of the [operative contract](#the-operative-contract-moved-out-rf2-ps7ia).

## Custody and amendment

**The tracked files here are the authoritative copies, and the only ones.** `decision-brief.md`, `specification.md` and everything under `lanes/` are amended in place, under beads, by whoever a bead sends. There is no upstream original, no republication, and nothing that can overwrite an edit made here. Every other file in this directory always worked that way and still does — this README and the records listed above, each maintained under its own beads by the owner its opening names. The five documents that [moved out](#the-operative-contract-moved-out-rf2-ps7ia) keep the same rule at their new address, with one addition: a gate reds on the same PR if a row goes missing.

That was ruled on 2026-08-16 under `rf2-rbme3`, retiring the snapshot contract this section used to carry. The evidence was one-way traffic: in the six days after publication, 42 reviewed and CI-gated commits amended the copies while all fifteen sources stayed byte-identical to the manifest above. A republication would therefore have restored superseded law over reviewed work — including an operator ruling — and the digest comparison, which runs source-against-manifest and never copy-against-copy, would have reported agreement afterwards.

**Interim custody is over. This directory is now the permanent home of what remains — and only of what remains.** It was an interim home from `rf2-rbme3` (2026-08-16) until the operator ruled the destination split on 2026-08-21 under `rf2-ps7ia`. The ruling is a four-way split, and this section records it as executed rather than as pending:

1. **Teaching and how-to** merges into the main published guide at [`docs/core/hicasso/`](../../../core/hicasso/index.md), which is already a first-class section in the MkDocs nav. Content merges INTO those pages; no file relocates as itself. **Executed on 2026-08-21**, and it was a two-chapter change rather than a sweep — see [the teaching set](#the-teaching-set-merged-into-the-guide) below for what was merged, what the guide already carried, and why every source file stayed.
2. **The operative contract** — the machine-gated documents — moved to [`implementation/hicasso/spec/`](../../../../implementation/hicasso/spec/README.md), beside the artefact, per the `tools/*/spec/` convention. [The five and their gates](#the-operative-contract-moved-out-rf2-ps7ia) are listed above. Their gates re-pointed in the same PR.
3. **Framework-level touchpoints** reach root [`spec/`](../../../../spec/README.md) only as freshly authored text, on demand, where re-frame2's own pattern is affected. **Nothing relocates into root `spec/`**: the 004D precedent stands, and product prose is never re-aimed into pattern specification.
4. **Governance and evidence** — the decision brief, the kill table and `validation.md`, the phase records, `lanes/`, `pilots/`, and the whole `studio/` tree — stay working design records at their existing addresses. Studio is evidence whose history is the content; it cannot graduate and must not be rewritten.

`docs/design/**` is still excluded from the published site, so nothing written here reaches a reader of the guide — which is the point for a working record and was the problem for a contract. Cite these paths as stable now. If the operator later wants a dedicated archive home for records, that is a separate call and nothing here forces it.

**One fence, carried forward from the retired contract.** A substantive semantic amendment to a file in this directory names its authorizing bead or ruling *at the site* — the dated beside-amendment every amending commit here already writes, in the shape *"this row read X until 2026-08-15 (`rf2-0fd3b`)"*. It is what lets the next reader tell a considered change from a drive-by one without leaving the page, and it is why a page that contradicts its own history is a defect rather than a mystery. Ordinary corrections — a typo, a broken link, a stale path — need no ceremony.

**No machinery, and that is deliberate.** Nothing generates, mirrors, synchronizes or drift-checks these files, and nothing should: the discipline is that they are edited under beads and reviewed like code. Note which gates do and do not reach them — `docs/design/**` sits outside the `mkdocs build --strict` site build, so it is never the gate for a change here; `scripts/check_doc_slugs.py` validates this tree's link targets and heading anchors, and `scripts/check_provenance_pins.py` validates changed pages under `docs/design/hicasso/`. Nothing validates a table's rendering or its column count, so whoever edits one verifies it by hand and says so.
